Portal workflow (analyst)

  1. Incidents — filter by case_id, open detail, generate report
  2. Ingest & evidence — parsing status, target index
  3. Threat Intel — OpenCTI/MISP IOC + ti_match correlation
  4. HELK / Velociraptor — hunting, playbook collection, TS export
  5. Forensic reports — collect, local AI, HTML/MD export

IOCs & bootstrap

12 IOCs are loaded into OpenSearch TI, OpenCTI, and MISP via --bootstrap-only. Without it, TI correlations may be missing after a CTI reset.
