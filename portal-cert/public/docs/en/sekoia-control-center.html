The Sekoia Control Center (v2.2) is the full management plane for your Sekoia.io SIEM: editable inventories, real-time ingestion monitoring, event search, CTI federation, advanced analytics and CERT automation — far beyond the standard Sekoia console capabilities.

Ingestion monitoring

The platform ingests local telemetry into OpenSearch (forensic-sekoia-telemetry-*) which powers:

  • Volumetry per intake, per source and per log.hostname over 1 h to 7 d windows;
  • Top hostnames per intake with last-event timestamps;
  • Automatic alerts: volume drop, silent intake, missing hostname, ingestion or parsing anomaly;
  • Acknowledgement of alerts by fingerprint, traced in the audit log;
  • Dedicated Grafana dashboard Sekoia Ingestion (volumes, eps, alerts).

Prerequisites

  • SEKOIA_API_KEY (read + write rights on intakes / rules / playbooks) in .env;
  • Optional CTI connectors: OPENCTI_TOKEN, MISP_KEY, THEHIVE_API_KEY, CORTEX_API_KEY — unconfigured sources are simply flagged "not configured";
  • End-to-end validation: ./scripts/validate-sekoia.sh.
